Tadawa Email Signature

Click the button, then paste it into Zoho Mail. Takes 2 minutes.

Step 1 — Copy the code

The signature code will be copied to your clipboard.

Step 2 — Paste into Zoho Mail

  1. Open Zoho Mail in your browser (mail.zoho.com).
  2. Click the gear icon (top right) → Settings.
  3. Go to Mail → Signatures → click + Add Signature.
  4. Name it "Tadawa".
  5. In the signature editor toolbar, click the < > button (Source / HTML view).
  6. Press Ctrl+V to paste.
  7. Click the < > button again to return to normal view — you should see the logo and signature.
  8. Replace [NAME], [JOB TITLE], and [PHONE] with your details.
  9. Below the editor, tick BOTH:
    • ✅ Set as default for new emails
    • ✅ Use for reply / forward
  10. Click Save. Send yourself a test email to confirm it works.
Critical: You MUST paste into the HTML/Source view (the < > button). If you paste into the normal editor, the signature will not work.
Forgetting "Use for reply/forward"? Your signature won't appear on replies — only brand new emails. Tick BOTH checkboxes.
